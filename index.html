02 / CARBON FOOTPRINT

Know how electricity connects to emissions.

The dashboard estimates electricity-related carbon by multiplying grid electricity by a regional emissions factor. That factor depends on location and reporting period.

642 kWh×0.73 kg CO₂e/kWh=≈ 469 kg CO₂e

Illustrative example only. The demo factor and kWh are assumptions, not live measurements. Solar generation is shown separately and is not subtracted from this estimate.
