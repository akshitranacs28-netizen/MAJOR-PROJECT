See how the demo works.

The dashboard brings household energy estimates, carbon calculations, and practical guidance together. Here's what is live in the demo and what requires a real connection.

From energy data to an estimate

01 · Source

Start with readings

The published demo uses sample household and appliance data. Accurate results need meter readings for your home and the right dates.

02 · Calculate

Apply your assumptions

Estimated bill uses kWh times the tariff. Estimated electricity carbon uses kWh times the grid emissions factor.

03 · Improve

Compare your changes

Use consistent meter readings to see whether a change affected consumption. The dashboard does not claim measured savings from the sample data.

What is connected right now?

DEMO DATA

Sample household

Displayed readings and bills are illustrative defaults. You can change tariff and grid factor in dashboard settings, but the demo does not read a physical meter by itself.

WATT COACH

Advice depends on mode

Local guidance uses simple household-aware rules. Live AI replies require a configured server backend and must be enabled; the provider key stays on the server.

DEVICE LINK

No hardware is paired

The appliance pairing flow is simulated. Google Home is an integration roadmap here; this web demo does not connect to or control a Google Home device.
