Small changes, measured clearly

Save energy without guessing.

Start with a real baseline, try one practical change, then compare similar days on your meter. Watt can help you choose what to investigate first.

Start with the parts of home you can observe

The best first step depends on your home, climate, equipment, and tariff. These ideas are prompts to investigate, not guaranteed savings.

01 / COOLING

Review cooling routines

Check thermostat schedules and room use. Clean filters as the manufacturer recommends, close doors or curtains when useful, and compare meter readings across similar weather.

02 / HOT WATER

Look at water heating

Notice when the heater runs and whether hot water is needed then. Use built-in timers if available and follow the appliance maker's instructions.

03 / STANDBY

Find always-on loads

Check devices that stay on when idle. A plug-in energy meter can help identify loads before you decide whether changing their use makes sense.

Make a fair before-and-after comparison

01 · Baseline

Record actual use

Use the same meter and a consistent time interval. Note unusual weather, guests, or changes in occupancy.

02 · Change

Try one adjustment

Change a single routine so you can tell which action may have affected the reading.

03 · Review

Check the result

Compare kWh and cost using your real tariff. If conditions differ, treat the result as an estimate.
